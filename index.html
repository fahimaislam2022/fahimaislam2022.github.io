About

Research with practical impact

AI for healthcare, hands-on engineering, and service to the community.

I study Computer Science and Engineering at AIUB in Dhaka and work remotely as a Research Assistant with Elite Research Lab PLC in Queens, New York. My research interests include AI for healthcare, health informatics, medical image analysis, computer vision, vision-language models, large language models, explainable AI, and trustworthy learning. I am fluent in English.

Current work includes auditing PCOS datasets for leakage and shortcut learning, and building a multimodal PCOS pipeline that combines ultrasound images with patient-matched clinical data. My academic thesis studies lightweight, semi-supervised polyp segmentation with temporal consistency.
